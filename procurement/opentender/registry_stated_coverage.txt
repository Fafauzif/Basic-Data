6. STATED DATA-QUALITY CAVEATS (page section "Data quality", quoted)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
"The dataset has quality issues to take into account, notably:" (line 411)
  - "The data has date typos, including dates up to 3020. The correct data
     include dates from 2008 till the current year only, so you may want to
     filter the incorrect dates." (line 413)
  - "A small number of non-unique combinations of `ocid` and `id` values."
     (line 414)
  - "Multiple entries in `parties` for the same organization with differing
     `id`s. In such cases, you may need to fallback to using `name` to
     disambiguate organizations." (line 415)
  - "A small number of contracting processes that do not declare a buyer."
     (line 416)
  - "A small number of missing supplier names." (line 417)
"The dataset has minor issues that should not interfere with most uses. They
include:" (line 419)
  - "The start and end date of the selection period are erroneously mapped to
     `planning.milestones.dueDate` and `planning.milestones.dateMet`
     respectively." (line 421)
